BATTERY CONFIGURATION
---------------------
Configuration: 3S7P
Total cells: 21 × 18650 Li-ion
Cell capacity: 2200 mAh
Nominal voltage: 11.1 V
Maximum voltage: 12.6 V
Total capacity: 15.4 Ah
Approx. energy: 171 Wh


BATTERY CELLS
-------------
21 × 18650 Li-ion cells
2200 mAh per cell
3.7 V nominal
4.2 V maximum


BMS
---
1 × 3S Li-ion BMS
Continuous discharge: 40 A
Cell balancing: Required

Required protections:
- Overcharge protection
- Over-discharge protection
- Over-current protection
- Short-circuit protection
- Cell balancing
- Temperature protection preferred


CHARGER
-------
1 × 12.6 V / 3 A Li-ion CC/CV charger

Output: 12.6 V
Maximum charging current: 3 A
Charging rate: approximately 0.2C


BATTERY MONITOR
---------------
1 × Battery voltage/capacity meter

Purpose:
- Battery voltage monitoring
- Approximate charge-level indication
- Quick battery health/status check


EXTERNAL BATTERY TERMINALS
--------------------------
2 × External high-current battery terminals

Terminal 1: BATTERY +
Terminal 2: BATTERY -

These terminals must be:
- Physically separated from each other
- Properly insulated
- Clearly labelled
- Mounted outside the robot chassis
- Protected against accidental short circuits
- Rated for the maximum expected battery current

Purpose:
- External battery voltage testing
- Battery diagnostic checking
- Emergency battery isolation/testing
- Maintenance access without opening the entire robot

IMPORTANT:
The external terminals should NOT bypass the BMS.

Recommended path:

Battery cells
     |
     v
   3S BMS
     |
     v
External BAT+ / BAT-
     |
     v
Main fuse / switch / robot power distribution
