Sub-Circuit 3: 4× HC-SR04 Ultrasonic Array & 5V-to-3.3V Protection Dividers
GPIO OVERVOLTAGE PROTECTION

The HC-SR04 operates at 5V logic. Connecting its 5V ECHO output pulse directly to an ESP32 GPIO pin (rated for 3.3V max) will degrade or destroy the microcontroller input buffer over time. A 1kΩ / 2kΩ voltage divider steps down the 5.0V signal to 3.33V safely.

Voltage Divider Schematic (Per Sensor)

HC-SR04 ECHO Pin (5.0V Pulse Output) │ [R1 = 1 kΩ Resistor] │ ├──────────────────────► To ESP32 GPIO (3.33V Input) │ [R2 = 2 kΩ Resistor] │ Common GND
$$V_{out} = V_{in} \times \frac{R_2}{R_1 + R_2} = 5.0\text{V} \times \frac{2\text{k}\Omega}{1\text{k}\Omega + 2\text{k}\Omega} = 3.33\text{V}$$

2. Firmware Loop & Obstacle Safety Execution Flow

flowchart TD
    START([System Power-On / Reset]) --> INIT[Initialize ESP32 GPIOs & LEDC PWM Timers @ 20kHz]
    INIT --> NET[Initialize ESP-NOW Peer Communication Protocol]
    NET --> LOOP[Enter Main FreeRTOS Real-Time Control Loop]

    LOOP --> RECV{ESP-NOW Packet Received?}
    RECV -- Yes --> PARSE[Extract Linear & Angular Velocity Commands]
    RECV -- No --> KEEP[Maintain Last Target Velocity]

    CHECK_DIST -- Yes (Rear Danger) --> STOP_REV[Override PWM: Inhibit Reverse Motion & Trigger Obstacle Alert]
    CHECK_DIST -- No (Path Clear) --> CALC_DIFF[Compute Differential Drive PWM for Left/Right BTS7960]

    STOP_FWD --> APPLY[Write LEDC PWM to GPIO 25/26 & 27/14]
    STOP_REV --> APPLY
    CALC_DIFF --> APPLY

    APPLY --> DELAY[Wait 10ms Task Tick Interval]
    DELAY --> LOOP
Isolated Power Distribution & Common Star Ground Architecture
NOISE & GROUND LOOP PREVENTION

High-power DC motors cause severe inductive switching noise, voltage spikes, and ground potential shifts when accelerating or reversing. The PRAYAS Motor Node isolates motor power from sensitive logic while ensuring a single point Star Ground Connection.

CRITICAL SAFETY & GROUNDING WARNING:
1. Do NOT use the metal robot frame/chassis as the electrical ground connection! An accidental wire short to the frame can cause destruction of the ESP32 or BTS7960 drivers.
2. All ground return lines (BTS7960 B-, Buck Converter In-, ESP32 GND, and HC-SR04 GNDs) MUST terminate directly at the heavy-gauge Battery Negative (-) Star Bus block.
3. Verify stall current before running twin motors in parallel! Two 12V 200 RPM Johnson motors in parallel demand high starting inrush current (~15A–25A total under stall). The BTS7960 heatsink must have adequate ventilation.
